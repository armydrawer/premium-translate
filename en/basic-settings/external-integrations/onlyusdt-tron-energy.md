## Module settings

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (875).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To configure the module, go to **"Orders - TRON Energy"**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (876).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**API Key** — enter the API key generated earlier in the OnlyUSDT bot.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (803).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Wallets (in new line)** — enter one or more of your TRON wallets used for manual payouts. Each wallet on a new line.

{% hint style="info" %}
💡 _You can add a name to each wallet using the `|` separator for easier navigation._

_Example: `TXxxx...xxx | Main wallet`_
{% endhint %}

## Purchasing energy

Rented energy is available temporarily — **30 minutes** from the moment of purchase.

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ _Rent energy immediately before making the actual payout from the wallet._
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (811).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To rent energy, select a wallet and a tariff, then click **"Apply"**.&#x20;

The amount of rented energy or an error with its reason will be displayed in the field above.

{% hint style="info" %}
Two tariffs are available:

<table><thead><tr><th width="106.20001220703125">Tariff</th><th width="118">Energy</th><th>Use case</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1</td><td>66,000</td><td>One USDT TRC20 transfer (~2.3 TRX)</td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td>135,000</td><td>Two transfers, or one transfer to a new wallet where USDT has never been used (requires twice the energy)</td></tr></tbody></table>
{% endhint %}

<details>

<summary>Error: "Address is not activated"</summary>

If you specify a wallet for payouts that has never been used before, you may receive the error **"address is not activated"**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (880).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To activate such an address, either send funds to it for future payouts, or use the dedicated button in the OnlyUSDT bot under the **"Energy"** section.

</details>

Energy rental is paid in TRX from your wallet balance in the OnlyUSDT bot.&#x20;

You can top up the wallet directly in the bot using TRX or USDT - go to **"Personal Account"** → **"Wallet"**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (881).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
